Precious Karley's Diaphragm Animation

In collaboration with The Definitive Human Project.

All 12 rib bones, The Manubrium, Sternum, Xiphoid process and Vertebrae T1-T12 were textured and retopologised in Zbrush.

New linkages in the coastal cartilage joining the ribs to chest bones were modelled in 3ds Max.


The Lungs are a segmented MRI dataset from 3D Slicer, modelled and textured in Zbrush.


The diapragm was modelled and textured in Zbrush using 2d anatomical references.
