Aortic Aneurysm Alert: An educational simulation.

This project was a successful collaboration between myself, collgues Laura Hudson and Dunja Rashkova. This application was designed to make learning about aortic aneurysms more visual and engaging. It allows users to explore a series of themed rooms with 3D anatomical models, animations, videos, interactive activities and a final quiz.
There are four rooms total, and I desinged three of them.


Room 1: What is an aortic aneurysm?

Room 2: Different types of Aortic Dissections

Room 3: Symptoms of an aortic Aneurysm
