THE SCAN: Evaluation of a Patient-Centred Multimedia Application

This mobile Application was developed in collaboration with NHS Greater Glasgow & Clyde. With this development, I utilised the psychoeducational effect of narrative to deliver information to patients attending Myocardial Perfusion appointments.

Patients attending Nuclear Cardiology departments for myocardial perfusion imaging may arrive with negative mental models of their anticipated appointment experience. These cognative representations are molded by prior experience, preappointment infomration and emotional states.


Leaflets are the standard method of preappointment information delivery for NHS patients, however Patients with suspected CAD often struggle with existential uncertainties and concerns that cannot be served by information alone.


During 3 months of clinical observation I witnessed the compassionate, service of the Advanced Technologist team at Glasgow Royal Infirmary NHSGGC. Through life drawings and gesture based sketches, I captured realistic depictions of the patient experience for the purposes of multimedia patient centred education.
